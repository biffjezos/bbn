**Status:** Not started. Deferred until multi-role support exists.

**Prerequisite:** A user account must be able to hold more than one role simultaneously (e.g. `admin` + `venue_manager`). Currently single role per account, so an admin cannot also be a `venue_manager`.

---

## Problem

When a `venue_manager` account is deleted, their venue(s) are currently cascade-deleted (T-06 decision). Data loss risk for venues that should persist under new management.

---

## Options

- **Option A:** Configurable "fallback manager" per venue (set by admin at creation time). Primary manager deletion → transfer to fallback.
- **Option B:** New interim role (`orphan_manager`) that can hold venue documents without appearing on the map. Admin reassigns from the orphan pool.
- **Option C:** Venues soft-deleted / suspended for N days after manager deletion, giving admin time to reassign before permanent deletion.

No implementation until multi-role support is landed and a preferred option is chosen.
