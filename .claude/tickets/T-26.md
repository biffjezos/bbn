## The Problem

**Question:** Why is auth.rs having a 500m radius hardcoded? Why is auth coupled to any app setting? Why is meta_tiers missing the premium tier?

1. Why is `auth.rs` hardcoding 500 m?

It shouldn't be. `auth.rs` is an identity extractor — its job is to decode who you are, not what radius you get. The `GatewayRadii` struct was attached
to `GatewayIdentity` as a convenience shortcut: the gateway resolves radii during verification and injects them so downstream services don't need to call
authority-service themselves. That's the intent. The fallback of `500` was meant as "guest-level safe default" but that's the auth module making an
application policy decision. It has no business doing that. The correct fallback is `0` (unknown), exactly as I just changed it — signal "you don't know"
rather than "pretend it's guest."

2. Why is auth coupled to app config at all?

It's a layering violation. GatewayRadii inside GatewayIdentity mixes identity (who you are) with tier policy (what you can do). Those are two different

- GatewayIdentity carries: `sub`, `role`, `account_type`, `tier`, `tv`, `features`
- The `location-service` derives its own radius by calling `get_nearby_radius_m(&state, &identity.tier)` every time — which it already has,
  and which already caches with a 5-minute TTL

The `GatewayRadii` optimisation (pre-resolving at gateway) is premature and the added complexity produced this exact bug.
It should be ripped out, or at minimum never used in the fallback path.

3. Why might `meta_tiers` be missing "premium"?

`admin_list_tiers` seeds the collection from `static_tiers()` only when `count == 0`. If the collection was created with some records but not all four tiers,
it never reseeds. There's no integrity check — no assertion that every known tier name exists in `meta_tiers`. So a partial collection is silently accepted
and any missing tier falls back to 500 m.

The deeper problem: the tier definitions are split across two sources of truth — `static_tiers()` in Rust code and the meta_tiers MongoDB collection. load_tiers
merges them by preferring the DB, but only if the DB is non-empty. This means the DB can be partially wrong and nothing complains.

The right fix long-term is a ticket. Want me to create it? It would cover:

- Remove `GatewayRadii` from `GatewayIdentity` (location-service uses its own cached tier lookup, which it already has)
- Remove the `X-Auth-Radii` header injection from the gateway proxy
- Enforce `meta_tiers` completeness at authority-service startup (upsert all static tiers, not just insert-if-empty)
