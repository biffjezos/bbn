## Problem

The app's rate limits (login, guest session) have strict limits. The limit is logged to the console but the average user sees no notification, only an error. Since it affects only the login-, registration-modals and guest sessions, there should be a notification banner at the top of the map (just below the main menu bar) stating that the attempted action is rate-limited in the development environment — asking for support to open up the app and providing a link to /donate.

The notification banner component already exists — just needs the message wired up.
